# 🗣️ AI English Speaking Partner

An AI-powered English speaking practice platform designed to help students and job seekers improve their English communication skills through natural conversations, voice practice, job interview simulations, role play, grammar correction, and progress tracking.

The goal of this project is to provide a friendly AI speaking partner that users can practice with anytime, instead of using a traditional grammar-checking application.

---

## 🌐 Project Overview

The **AI English Speaking Partner** allows users to communicate with an AI in English through text and voice.

The AI understands the conversation context and provides natural responses while helping users improve their English.

The platform supports:

- 💬 Normal English conversations
- 🎤 Voice-based English practice
- 💼 Job interview practice
- 🎭 Role-play conversations
- ✍️ Grammar and vocabulary correction
- 📊 Speaking progress tracking
- 💾 MongoDB conversation history
- 🕘 Recent conversations
- 🔄 Continue previous conversations

---

## ✨ Features

### 💬 AI English Conversation

Users can have natural conversations with the AI.

The AI is designed to behave like a friendly English-speaking partner rather than simply correcting every sentence.

Example:

```text
User:
I went college yesterday.

AI:
That's nice! What did you do at college?

Correction:
"I went to college yesterday."

Explanation:
We use "to" before "college" when describing movement to a place.
